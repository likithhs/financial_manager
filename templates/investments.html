Core Asset Classes & Horizon Profiling

Non-speculative growth instruments
Nifty / Sensex Index Funds
Moderate Risk

Low-cost passive investment tracking India's top 50 bluechip companies. Ideal for consistent wealth compounding.

Recommended Horizon: 3 to 7+ Years
Expected Return: 11% – 14% CAGR
Primary Strength: Eliminates single-company stock picking risk.
Fixed Deposits & Debt Funds
Low Risk

Guaranteed returns backed by scheduled commercial banks and government debt securities. Perfect for emergency buffers.

Recommended Horizon: 6 Months to 3 Years
Expected Return: 6.5% – 7.5% Annual
Primary Strength: 100% capital preservation & predictable interest.
Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB)
Low to Moderate

Government securities denominated in grams of gold. Combines gold price appreciation with a guaranteed 2.5% annual payout.

Recommended Horizon: 5 to 8 Years
Expected Return: Gold Market Rate + 2.5%
Primary Strength: Zero making charges and tax-free upon maturity.
Diversified Equity Mutual Funds
Moderate Risk

Actively managed portfolios allocating dynamically across Large-cap, Mid-cap, and Small-cap growth companies.

Recommended Horizon: 5+ Years
Expected Return: 12% – 16% Long Term
Primary Strength: Professional fund management and sector rotation.
Public Provident Fund (PPF)
Zero Risk

Government-backed statutory savings scheme with complete EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) tax benefits and guaranteed returns.

Recommended Horizon: 15 Years (Lock-in)
Expected Return: 7.1% Compounded Annually
Primary Strength: Sovereign backing & total tax exemption.
Direct Bluechip Equities
High Volatility

Direct shares in established market leaders (e.g. TCS, Reliance, Infosys, HDFC Bank) with proven balance sheets.

Recommended Horizon: 3 to 10+ Years
Expected Return: Market Driven + Dividends
Primary Strength: Direct ownership and voting rights.
